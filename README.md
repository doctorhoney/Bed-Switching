\## 🇬🇧 English



\### About



Bed Switching is a Minecraft mod that allows players to save multiple respawn points and choose between them directly from the death screen.



Instead of being limited to your last bed, you can manage your bases, respawn anchors and death history through a convenient interface.



\### Features



\- Save multiple beds and respawn anchors.

\- Choose your respawn point from the death screen.

\- Rename and remove saved locations.

\- View coordinates, dimensions and biomes.

\- Colored bed icons.

\- Sort respawn points by distance, name or registration order.

\- View the distance from your death location.

\- Personal history of your last 20 deaths.

\- Copy coordinates to the clipboard.

\- Delete individual death records or clear the history.

\- English and Russian localization.

\- Singleplayer and multiplayer support.

\- Optimized chunk loading and server-side validation.



\### Requirements



\- Minecraft 1.21.11

\- NeoForge 21.11.48 or newer compatible version

\- Java 21



\### Installation



1\. Install NeoForge for Minecraft 1.21.11.

2\. Download Bed Switching from CurseForge or Modrinth.

3\. Place the JAR file into your `mods` folder.

4\. Launch Minecraft.



For multiplayer, install the same version of the mod on both the client and server.



\### Building from source



Clone or download this repository.



Windows:



`gradlew.bat build`



Linux / macOS:



`./gradlew build`



The compiled JAR will be available in `build/libs/`.

\### О моде



Bed Switching — мод для Minecraft, позволяющий сохранять несколько точек возрождения и выбирать между ними прямо на экране смерти.



Теперь необязательно возрождаться только на последней кровати. Управляйте своими базами, якорями возрождения и историей смертей через удобный интерфейс.
